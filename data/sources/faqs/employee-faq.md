# Employee FAQ

## When is payday?

Acme pays semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last business day of each month. If the 15th falls on a weekend, pay is issued the prior Friday.

## How do I update my direct deposit or tax withholding?

In the HR Portal (go/hr) under **Pay > Payment Methods**. Changes made before the 5th or the 20th apply to the next paycheck.

## What is the learning & development budget?

Each employee has $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, and conferences. Submit through the expense tool under the category "Learning". Unused budget does not roll over.

## How do I get a new monitor or equipment?

File a request in the IT Service Desk. Standard equipment (monitor, keyboard, mouse, headset) ships in 3-5 business days. Home office stipend is a one-time $500 for new hires.

## Who do I contact about benefits?

Email benefits@acme.example or post in #ask-people. Open enrollment runs November 3-17, 2026.

## How does the referral program work?

Submit referrals in the Recruiting tool. If your referral is hired and stays 90 days, you receive the bonus: $5,000 for engineering roles (effective October 15, 2026) and $2,000 for all other roles.
