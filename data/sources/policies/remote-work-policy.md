# Remote Work Policy

## Who it applies to

All full-time employees. Acme is remote-friendly with hubs in Austin, New York, and Lisbon.

## Hub employees

- Employees living within 50 miles of a hub are **hub employees** and are expected in the office 2 days per week (Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days).
- Exceptions require manager and People Team approval.

## Fully remote employees

- Employees outside hub radius work fully remote.
- Company covers coworking space up to $250/month if requested.
- Expect to travel to a hub up to 2 times per year for team gatherings (paid by the company).

## Working from another country

- You may work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Submit a request in the HR Portal at least 2 weeks ahead so Legal can check tax and immigration requirements.
- Working from sanctioned countries is not allowed.

## Core hours

Everyone should be available 10:00-14:00 in their team's primary time zone.
