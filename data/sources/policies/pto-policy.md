# Time Off Policy

## Vacation

- Acme has flexible PTO with a **minimum** of 15 days per year that everyone is expected to take.
- Request time off in the HR Portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 3 days or more.
- On-call engineers must arrange a swap before taking PTO during their rotation.

## Company holidays (US, 2026)

New Year's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving (Thursday and Friday), Christmas Eve, Christmas Day. Plus a company-wide **Recharge Week** from December 28 to December 31.

## Sick leave

- Unlimited sick days for short illnesses; no doctor's note needed for under 5 consecutive days.

## Parental leave

- 16 weeks fully paid for all parents, usable within 12 months of birth or adoption.

## Volunteer time

- 2 paid volunteer days per year.
