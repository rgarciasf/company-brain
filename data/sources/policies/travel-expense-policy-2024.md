# Travel & Expense Policy (2024)

## Meals

- Daily meal per diem: **$75** per day for domestic travel, $100 for international.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Flights

- Economy class for all flights. Premium economy allowed for flights over 8 hours.
- Book at least 14 days in advance when possible.

## Hotels

- Up to $250 per night in major cities, $175 elsewhere.

## Submitting expenses

- Submit within 60 days of the expense with itemized receipts for anything over $25.
- Manager approval required for all expenses.
