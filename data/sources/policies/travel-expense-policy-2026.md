# Travel & Expense Policy (2026)

Effective **July 1, 2026**. Supersedes the 2024 Travel & Expense Policy.

## Meals

- Daily meal per diem: **$90** per day for domestic travel, **$120** for international.
- Client dinners: up to $150 per person with a VP-approved business purpose. Alcohol is reimbursable only at client dinners, up to 2 drinks per person.

## Flights

- Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class requires CFO approval.
- Book at least 21 days in advance when possible. Use the travel tool (Navan) for all bookings.

## Hotels

- Up to $300 per night in tier-1 cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo), $200 elsewhere.

## Ground transport

- Rideshare and taxis are reimbursable. Personal car mileage at the IRS rate.

## Submitting expenses

- Submit within **30 days** of the expense (reduced from 60). Itemized receipts required for anything over $25.
- Expenses under $75 are auto-approved; over $75 require manager approval.
- Late submissions over 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Q4 2026 note

Internal team offsites should be virtual during Q4 2026 unless approved by a VP (per leadership offsite decision).
