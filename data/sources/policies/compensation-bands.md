# Engineering Compensation Bands 2026 (Managers only)

Confidential. Do not share with individual contributors.

## Base salary bands (US, Tier 1 cities)

| Level | Title | Band |
|---|---|---|
| E3 | Software Engineer | $135,000 - $165,000 |
| E4 | Senior Software Engineer | $170,000 - $210,000 |
| E5 | Staff Engineer | $215,000 - $260,000 |
| E6 | Principal Engineer | $265,000 - $320,000 |

## Adjustments

- Tier 2 US locations: 90% of Tier 1 band. Lisbon hub: 60% of Tier 1 band (paid in EUR).
- Merit cycle happens each March. Off-cycle raises need VP and People Team approval.
- Equity refresh grants are reviewed annually in the merit cycle.
