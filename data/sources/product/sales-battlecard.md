# Sales Battlecard: Pulse vs Competitors

Internal only. For Sales and Customer Success.

## vs Amplitude

- Win on: unlimited dashboard seats, EU data residency, price (Growth is ~35% cheaper at 25M events).
- Lose on: depth of behavioral cohort tooling. Don't claim parity on predictive cohorts.

## vs Mixpanel

- Win on: query speed (95% under 2s), warehouse sync on Enterprise.
- Lose on: brand awareness in early-stage startups. Lead with the Startup Program (Growth free for year one).

## vs PostHog

- Win on: enterprise readiness (SCIM, 99.9% SLA, SOC 2 Type II).
- Lose on: self-hosting. We have no on-prem option until 2027; do not promise a date.

## Objection handling

- "AI features?" -> AI Insights beta Nov 12, 2026; Enterprise includes it, Growth is +$49/month.
- "Too expensive?" -> Offer annual billing ($399 vs $479 monthly) before discounting.
