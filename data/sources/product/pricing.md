# Pulse Pricing & Plans

| Plan | Price | Included events / month | Overage | Data retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 1 million | Not available (hard cap) | 3 months |
| Growth | $399/month (billed annually) or $479 monthly | 25 million | $12 per extra million | 13 months |
| Enterprise | Custom (typically from $36,000/year) | Custom | Negotiated | 25 months |

## Add-ons

- **AI Insights**: $49/month on Growth; included in Enterprise. Not available on Free.
- **Extra data retention**: +12 months for $150/month (Growth).

## Discounts

- Nonprofits and education: 50% off Growth.
- Startups (under 2 years old, under $5M raised): Growth free for the first year via the Startup Program.
- Sales reps may discount Enterprise up to 15% without approval; 15-25% needs the VP Sales; over 25% needs the CFO.

## Feature availability

- SSO (SAML/OIDC): Growth and Enterprise
- SCIM: Enterprise only
- 99.9% uptime SLA: Enterprise only
- Warehouse sync: Enterprise only
