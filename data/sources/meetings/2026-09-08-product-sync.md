# Product Sync - September 8, 2026

**Attendees:** Dana Ruiz, Lena Okafor, Tom Becker, Aisha Khan, Sofia Marin

## Decisions

- **AI Insights** will launch as a beta on November 12, 2026 for Growth and Enterprise customers. Free plan is excluded at launch.
- The legacy **Reports v1** feature will be deprecated. Customers get 90 days notice starting October 15; removal on January 13, 2027.
- Pricing for AI Insights: included in Enterprise; $49/month add-on for Growth.

## Discussion

- Tom raised that Reports v1 is still used by roughly 340 workspaces, mostly on the Growth plan. Customer Success will run a migration campaign to Dashboards v2.
- Aisha noted that the mobile app crash rate on Android 15 is 1.8%, above the 1% target. Fix is prioritized for release 4.3.
- Sofia wants AI Insights to respect workspace permissions; no insights from dashboards a user can't access. Agreed as a launch requirement.
